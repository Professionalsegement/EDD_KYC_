# AML / KYC / EDD Compliance Monitoring Dashboard

## Overview

This project is an AML (Anti-Money Laundering), KYC (Know Your Customer), and EDD (Enhanced Due Diligence) Compliance Monitoring Dashboard developed in Power BI.

The dashboard provides a centralized view of customer due diligence, AML alerts, suspicious activity reporting, risk assessment, and transaction monitoring. It is designed to help compliance teams monitor financial crime risks and support regulatory compliance processes.

---

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this dashboard is to:

- Monitor customer KYC status
- Track AML alerts and investigations
- Manage Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) cases
- Record and review Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs)
- Assess customer risk levels
- Monitor flagged transactions
- Support compliance and regulatory reporting

- Prevent money laundering
